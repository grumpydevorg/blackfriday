    Connecting to the arena…

    What runs where tonight
    • Web Function: this page, every phone and the game API. Start and Close are presenter-only calls to it.
    • Durable Function: the game's memory, one execution per game. It waits for each Close without running or billing anything, then plans, scores and publishes the round.
    • Event Function: the judge. Five runs, all before Round 1: Launch, two migrations, two cart checks. Each one drives a MicroVM.
    • MicroVMs: the real PHP shop and PostgreSQL, one tiny VM per stage, with no AWS role, destroyed when the stage ends.
    • Managed Instances: not deployed. Christmas's "Commit" option is priced like it: modelled, not measured.

    How this round uses Lambda

    The customer rules
    • New customers arrive every round: the forecast times the market draw, give or take 10% per shop. Last round's returning customers come too.
    • A shop serves them fast up to its capacity, slowly in a narrow band above that, and turns the rest away.
    • Every verified sale, fast or slow, earns $10. Profit is $10 × sales − the shop's modelled AWS cost − refunds.
    • Next round, 75% of fast customers come back, 50% of slow ones and 20% of those turned away. Only 5% of anyone whose cart was lost or who was charged twice.
