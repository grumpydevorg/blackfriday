# Artwork

Concept art for BLACK FRIDAY, generated with ChatGPT image generation in
November 2026 and selected by Rick Morgans.

| File | Used for |
|:--|:--|
| `cloud-surge.png` | Title slide and the arena background |
| `four-rounds.png` | The four rounds of the quarter |
| `architecture-showdown.png` | Teams comparing shop designs |
| `arena-dashboard.png` | What a spectator arena could look like |
| `ui-asset-kit.png` | Colours, controls and icons for the arena and slides |

The images are illustrations. Their scores, prices, traffic figures and
service choices are invented by the image generator: they are not game rules
or measured results. The rules are in [`docs/game.md`](../docs/game.md).
